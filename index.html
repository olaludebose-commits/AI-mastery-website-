08 — GOOD TO KNOW

Ask us something else ↗
Is the current training programme free?+

Yes. The current AI MASTERY training programme is free. Future programmes may have different fees, which will be communicated before registration.

Do I need previous AI experience?+

No. The learning paths are designed to be beginner-friendly. Curiosity and willingness to practise are a great place to start.

When are the sessions held?+

The current training schedule is every Saturday, delivered online. Specific session details will be shared with accepted learners.

Will I receive a certificate?+

Eligible students may receive an AI MASTERY course-completion certificate after meeting the stated requirements. It is not a government-accredited qualification.

Can I learn using a phone?+

Many learning activities can be accessed on a smartphone, although some coding and development tasks may be easier with a laptop or desktop computer.

YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy policy.

AI MASTERY collects the information you submit through applications, account registration, and contact forms to manage training, communicate with learners, and support learning services. We do not publish student application details. Access to private records should be restricted by authenticated roles and database policies.

Do not submit sensitive personal information that is not needed for training. You may contact us at olaludeisaackehinde@gmail.com to ask about your submitted information. Data handling also depends on the hosting, authentication, and database providers configured for this website.

Security and retention

Passwords must be handled by the authentication provider, never stored directly by the website. Access controls and Row Level Security should be enabled before collecting real student data. Records should be retained only as needed for training administration and applicable obligations.
